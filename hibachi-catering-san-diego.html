Frequently asked questions

Planning hibachi in San Diego

Which San Diego areas do you serve?

We serve San Diego, La Jolla, Del Mar, Carlsbad, Encinitas, Chula Vista and nearby communities, subject to date and address availability.

How much is hibachi catering?

Adults are $50 per person and choose two proteins. Children ages 4–12 are $25 and choose one protein. Children age 3 and under eat free. A $500 food minimum applies.

Can you cook at a vacation rental?

Yes, if the property allows it and there is a safe outdoor or well-ventilated cooking area. Please confirm the host or property rules first.

What do I need to provide?

Please provide tables and chairs, plates and utensils, drinks, and a clear, safe cooking area. We bring the chef, food, grill and cooking equipment.

Is a deposit required?

No deposit is required. We confirm availability, travel fee and your final quote after you submit your event details.
