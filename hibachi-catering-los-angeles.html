Frequently asked questions

Planning hibachi in Southern California

How much is hibachi catering?

Adults are $50 per person and choose two proteins. Children ages 4–12 are $25 and choose one protein. Children age 3 and under eat free. A $500 food minimum applies.

Do you serve Palm Springs and San Diego?

Yes. Los Angeles, Palm Springs and San Diego are our key Southern California service areas. Send us your event date and full address to confirm availability.

How are travel fees handled?

Travel fees vary by event location and will be confirmed with you before your booking is finalized.

What do I need to provide?

You provide tables and chairs for your guests, plates and utensils, drinks, and a clear area where we can safely set up and cook. We bring the chef, food, grill and cooking equipment.

Can you cook at an Airbnb or vacation rental?

Yes, if the property allows it and there is a safe cooking area. Please confirm the host or property rules before booking.

What happens if it rains?

Please provide a covered outdoor area, tent, patio or another safe, well-ventilated space. Contact us before the event if weather may affect your setup.

How do I confirm my reservation?

Submit your date, location and guest details through our booking page. We will confirm availability, travel fee and your final quote. No deposit required.
