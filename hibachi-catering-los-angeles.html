Frequently asked questions

Planning hibachi in Los Angeles

How much is hibachi catering?

Adults are $50 per person and choose two proteins. Children ages 4–12 are $25 and choose one protein. Children age 3 and under eat free. A $500 food minimum applies.

Which Los Angeles areas do you serve?

We serve Los Angeles, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Long Beach, the San Gabriel Valley, Orange County and nearby communities, depending on the date and exact event address.

What do I need to provide?

Please provide guest tables and chairs, plates and utensils, drinks, and a clear, safe cooking area. We bring the chef, food, grill and cooking equipment.

Can you cook at an Airbnb?

Yes, if the property allows it and has a safe cooking area. Please confirm the host or property rules before booking.

How do I confirm my reservation?

Submit your date, address and guest count. We will confirm availability, travel fee and the final quote. No deposit required.
