We bring the chef, hibachi grill, food and cooking equipment. You provide tables and chairs for your guests, plates and utensils, drinks, and a clear area where we can safely set up and cook.

What happens if it rains?

Hibachi is best enjoyed outdoors. If rain is expected, please provide a covered outdoor area, tent, patio or another safe, well-ventilated space. Contact us before the event if weather may affect your setup.

How much space do you need?

We need a flat, clear and well-ventilated cooking area with enough room around the grill for the chef to work safely. Please keep the setup away from low ceilings, flammable materials and heavy foot traffic.
