FAQ

Good to Know Before We Grill

What do I need to provide for the event?

We bring the chef, hibachi grill, food and cooking equipment. You provide tables and chairs for your guests, plates and utensils, drinks, and a clear area where we can safely set up and cook.

What happens if it rains?

Hibachi is best enjoyed outdoors. If rain is expected, please provide a covered outdoor area, tent, patio or another safe, well-ventilated space. Contact us before the event if weather may affect your setup.

How much space do you need?

We need a flat, clear and well-ventilated cooking area with enough room around the grill for the chef to work safely. Please keep the setup away from low ceilings, flammable materials and heavy foot traffic.

How does the $500 food minimum work?

Our minimum food charge is $500 per event. If your guest total and selected food are below $500, the food charge will still be $500. Add-ons and applicable travel fees are additional.

How is the travel fee calculated?

Travel is calculated from the O'Hare area: 0–5 miles is free, 5–50 miles is $50, and travel beyond 50 miles is $1 per mile. Your final travel fee is confirmed with your quote.

When and how do I pay?

After we confirm your event details and final quote, a $100 deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance is due at the end of your event. Payment is cash only. Your final quote will clearly show the food, add-ons, travel fee, applicable tax, deposit, and remaining balance.
