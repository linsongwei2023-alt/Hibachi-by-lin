Frequently asked questions

Planning hibachi in Southwest Michigan

Can you cook at a vacation rental?

Often, yes, if the property permits outside vendors and provides a safe, suitable cooking area. Please confirm the rental rules first.

Do you have a Michigan restaurant or office?

No. Hibachi by Lin is a mobile private hibachi service traveling from the Chicago area for confirmed events.

What do you bring to the event?

We bring the chef, hibachi grill, food and cooking equipment needed for the service and show.

How do I check menu choices and travel?

See the current choices on our menu page and enter the property address on the booking page.
