Why at-home hibachi works

A private hibachi experience without taking the party to a restaurant

At-home hibachi keeps your group together in your own space while adding live cooking and interaction to the event. It works especially well for birthdays, backyard gatherings, family celebrations, bachelor and bachelorette parties, and private dinners.

Your place

Built-in entertainment

The cooking itself becomes part of the party, giving guests something to watch and enjoy together.
