Planning hibachi in Chicago & Chicagoland

Do you provide mobile hibachi service in Naperville and other Chicago suburbs?

Yes. Hibachi by Lin brings hibachi at home and private hibachi chef service to confirmed event locations across Chicagoland, including Naperville, Schaumburg, Evanston, Oak Park, Skokie and Arlington Heights.

What kinds of parties are good for mobile hibachi?

Mobile hibachi is a popular choice for birthday parties, graduation parties, backyard gatherings, family celebrations and other private events.

We bring the private chef, hibachi grill, food, cooking equipment and entertainment. Guests provide seating, plates, utensils, drinks and a safe cooking area.
