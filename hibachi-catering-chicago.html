Planning hibachi in Chicago

Do you come to homes in Chicago and the suburbs?

Yes. Hibachi by Lin is a mobile service that brings the chef, food, grill, cooking equipment and live show to confirmed event locations across Chicago and Chicagoland.

What do you bring to the event?

We bring the private chef, hibachi grill, food, cooking equipment and entertainment. Guests provide seating, plates, utensils, drinks and a safe cooking area.
